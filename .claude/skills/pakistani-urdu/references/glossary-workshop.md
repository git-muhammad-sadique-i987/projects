# Motorcycle and workshop glossary (Pakistan)

How the words are actually said in Pakistani workshops and bike markets. The right-hand
column is what to write; "avoid" lists what makes it sound translated or Indian. Entries
marked *(check)* vary by city or shop; confirm with the person you are writing for when it
matters.

## People and places

| English | Write | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| mechanic (addressing him) | استاد جی | respectful default; also مستری |
| apprentice | چھوٹا | as in "چھوٹے کو کہہ دیں" |
| lathe operator / machinist | خرادیہ | his shop: خراد |
| welder | ویلڈنگ والا | |
| seat maker / upholsterer | سیٹ والا، گدی والا | |
| spare parts market | پرزوں کی مارکیٹ | use the local market's name if you know it |

## Measurements

Mechanics talk in inches and fractions: آدھا انچ، پون انچ، سوا انچ، ڈیڑھ انچ. Use mm for
vernier readings and give the inch equivalent: "15 ملی میٹر (آدھے انچ سے تھوڑا زیادہ)".
Clearance is "نیچے سے اونچائی" or "زمین سے فاصلہ".
